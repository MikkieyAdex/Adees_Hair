Hair Care Tips

Proper hair care is essential to maintain the health and longevity of your hair extensions. Here are some tips to help you take care of your hair:

  • Washing: Use a sulfate-free shampoo and conditioner. Wash your hair gently and avoid scrubbing.
  • Drying: Pat your hair dry with a towel instead of rubbing it. Avoid using high heat on your extensions.
  • Brushing: Use a wide-tooth comb or a brush designed for extensions. Start from the ends and work your way up to avoid breakage.
  • Styling: Limit the use of heat styling tools. If you must use them, apply a heat protectant spray first.
  • Sleeping: Tie your hair in a loose braid or ponytail before sleeping to prevent tangling.
  • Products: Avoid heavy oils and products that can weigh down your extensions. Opt for lightweight, nourishing products.

By following these tips, you can ensure that your hair extensions remain beautiful and last longer.
